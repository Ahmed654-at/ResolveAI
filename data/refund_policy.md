# Refund Policy

We want every customer to be happy with their purchase. This policy explains when a refund
is issued straight away and when a member of our team reviews the request first.

## Refund window

You can request a refund within **30 days of delivery**. Day 30 still counts. The window starts
on the date the order was delivered, not the date it was ordered. Requests made after day 30
are reviewed by our team, who may still approve them in special cases.

## Refund amount

Refunds of **$100 or less** can be issued straight away. Refunds over $100 are always reviewed
by a member of our team before they are approved. Refunds are for the amount paid for the order
and are returned to the original payment method.

## Orders that have not arrived

If your order is still on its way (shipped) or has been reported lost, it has not been
delivered yet, so the 30-day window has not started. These requests are reviewed by our team,
who check with the carrier before issuing a refund or a replacement.

## Repeat refunds

Customers who have had **3 or more approved refunds in the last 90 days** have any new refund
request reviewed by our team. This does not mean the request will be rejected. It only means
a person checks it first.

## Fraud and security checks

Every request goes through an automatic risk check. It looks at things like account age,
order value and refund history. Requests with a high risk score are reviewed by our team
before any money is returned.

## Orders already refunded

Each order can be refunded only once. If an order has already been refunded, a new request for
the same order is sent to our team instead of being paid again.

## Automatic refunds

A refund is issued straight away only when **all** of these are true:

- the refund is $100 or less
- the order was delivered within the last 30 days
- the order passes the fraud and security check
- you have had fewer than 3 approved refunds in the last 90 days
- the order has not been refunded before

## Human review

Any request that does not meet every condition above is sent to a member of our team.
Reviews usually take 1 to 2 business days. You will be told whether the refund was approved
or rejected, and why.
